## Platform

web

## Stack

Single self-contained static HTML file (inline CSS/JS, Google Fonts only), published as a Claude artifact and later hostable on GitHub Pages.

## Users

Hiring managers and recruiters for infrastructure operations and security operations roles (NOC and SOC, both), typically arriving from a CV, LinkedIn or a job application and scanning for fit in a minute or two.

## Product Purpose

Personal website for Jack Barry. It presents his experience and makes it easy to contact him. Success is a hiring manager seeing credible, specific operational experience and reaching out by email or LinkedIn.

## Positioning

Jack works first-line on the service desk for Smart DCC, the government-licensed secure communications network behind Britain's 37M+ smart meters. He triages incidents daily and escalates to DCC's NOC and SOC. Few junior candidates can point to national critical infrastructure in their day-to-day work.

## Operating Context

Incident management vocabulary: ServiceNow, BMC Remedy, ITIL, SLAs, P1–P4 priorities, queue management, first-contact resolution, NOC/SOC escalation, GDPR-auditable records.
